Fashion Boutique

A women's fashion boutique in Beckenham, Christchurch

Made in the Shade is a women's clothing store on Colombo Street in Beckenham, Christchurch. The boutique brings together a wide selection of women's fashion labels under one roof, so you can browse many favourite names in a single visit.

On the rails you'll find labels including Matisse, Blackstone, Anne Mardell, Mardee, Revue, Vue, Optimum, Not Your Daughters Jeans, Pingpong, Annabel's Closet, Weekend, Lisette, L'Officiel, Bittermoon and Zaket & Plover, along with the winter and summer 2026 collections listed below.

To ask about a label, a collection or a particular piece, call the shop on 03 982 1759, call or text the mobile on 027 412 0086, or email ali@madeintheshadefashions.co.nz.
